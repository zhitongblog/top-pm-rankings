# 100 Products That Changed the World

> All scores by Claude (AI) · PRODUCTS · THE 100 THAT CHANGED THE WORLD
> https://doaipm.com/en/great-products/

Only commercial products that actually shipped and were actually used at scale. We judge the product itself — not the company’s market cap, not the founder’s myth. Six attributes, one OVR score, and deep-dives lighting up the list one product at a time.

## How the scores work

Each attribute is scored 0–99 and weighted into an overall OVR (Originality 20% · Scale 20% · Impact 15% · Experience 15% · Business 15% · Durability 15%). Ranking is OVR, descending. The big difference from the people board is durability: products die, and dead products cannot score well on that axis — the Model T, the Walkman and the Famicom all lose rank because of it, deliberately. All scores are produced by Claude, Anthropic’s AI model: doaipm sets the dimensions and rules, Claude scores independently. Disagree? Come argue.

ORG Originality 20% · SCL Scale 20% · IMP Impact 15% · EXP Experience 15% · BIZ Business 15% · DUR Durability 15%
