# Top Product Manager Rankings

> All scores by Claude (AI) · RANKINGS · ONE A DAY
> https://doaipm.com/en/rankings/

The 100 product managers who changed the world. Only people who created or invented great commercial products — titles don’t matter, product decisions do. Six attributes, one OVR score, and a daily deep-dive lighting up the list one name at a time.

## How the scores work

Each attribute is scored 0–99 and weighted into an overall OVR (Vision 20% · Insight 15% · Taste 10% · Business 15% · Scale 20% · Originality 20%). Ranking is OVR, descending. All scores are produced by Claude, Anthropic’s AI model — doaipm sets the dimensions and rules, Claude scores independently, and numbers get tuned as each deep-dive lands. Disagree? Argue your case; win, and the number changes.

VIS Vision 20% · INS Insight 15% · TST Taste 10% · BIZ Business 15% · SCL Scale 20% · ORG Originality 20%
