## How the scores work

Each attribute is scored 0–99 and weighted into an overall OVR:

| Attribute | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| VIS · Vision | 20% | Seeing the future others can't |
| INS · Insight | 15% | Depth of user understanding |
| TST · Taste | 10% | Judgment on detail and experience |
| BIZ · Business | 15% | Monetization and business-model design |
| SCL · Scale | 20% | How many lives the product reached |
| ORG · Originality | 20% | Defining new paradigms and categories |

Only people who created or invented great **commercial** products qualify — titles don't matter, product decisions do. We judge product decisions, not people; every entry rests on public facts. (One community exception already exists: Linus Torvalds, voted in over the commercial rule.)

## FAQ

**Why is X ranked above Y?** Ranking is strictly OVR, descending; ties keep editorial order. Check the six attributes before arguing the total.

**Why is Dario Amodei (Anthropic's CEO) only 69th?** Claude scored its own boss conservatively on purpose. A board that flatters its judge's employer is worth nothing.

**Who is No. 100?** The seat is reserved. The AI era finally lets anyone who can say clearly what they want actually build it — No. 100 is the next PM who changes the world. Possibly you.
